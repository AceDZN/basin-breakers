# Basin Breakers — פורצי האגן

An original tactile water puzzle, commissioned 31 October 2026. Visual thesis: sunlit limestone terraces, copper and ceramic machinery, and turquoise water visibly changing a dry catchment. The world occupies the canvas; short Hebrew instructions sit at its edges.

## First minute

An upstream reservoir waits behind an earthen plug. Drag the excavation tool across the plug, turn the blue gate wheel and release a finite pulse. The first tank fills and the dry orchard responds. On the next landscape the shortest trench crosses a courtyard: water splashes against a house instead of reaching supply. Rewind restores all water and machinery to the start while retaining the earth plan; bend the cut around the house and test again. A pulse takes seconds, a revised plan takes one gesture.

## Decisions and changing state

Hydraulic head determines surface flow on an editable grid rendered as a substantial 3D cutaway. Excavation and raising consume earth budget. A gate controls release rate, so narrow reaches and flood storage matter. Permeable patches move the same water and tracers into an aquifer store. Pumps withdraw only existing groundwater. Membranes split intake into freshwater and concentrated brine while spending energy; wastewater treatment retains removed contamination in sludge and preserves reuse provenance. A full drinking tank containing saline, contaminated or reclaimed water fails its quality objective. Farms and environmental outflows have their own requirements.

## Controls

Pointer/touch drag: excavate or raise terrain. Four world tools: cut, raise, gate and inspect. Select machinery to adjust its opening or operation. Space: plan/run. R: rewind pulse, preserve terrain plan. Escape: pause. F: fullscreen. Orbit with right drag or camera tool; wheel/pinch zoom; reset camera always available. Keyboard physical event codes work with Hebrew layouts. Touch actions have visible equivalents. Blur pauses time.

## Mistake, retry and win

A mistake has a visible physical cause: flooded courtyard, stranded water uphill, recharge loss into permeable soil, aquifer depletion, mixed brine or wrong destination. The cause caption states the relationship. Rewind is immediate. Winning requires actual modeled water at all required destinations, quality thresholds, flood safety and any aquifer/environment reserve at the end of the replay. No question gates or route answer keys.

## Campaign and replay

Six acts of four authored landscapes: drainage; land cover; groundwater; salinity; destination-specific reuse; integrated drought and storm transfer. All 24 remain selectable for practice. Progress saves locally without personal data; first unaided success and hinted success remain distinct. Replays preserve the plan and record earth/energy efficiency. Hints name a physical relationship without drawing a route; the last act provides fewer cues and changed topology.

## Camera, art and sound

Low oblique orthographic view with orbit/zoom, terraced mesh walls and visibly cut channels. Water has animated highlights, foam and wet margins; filling triggers plants, chimes and restrained celebratory motion. Tripo sluice is the identity prop; precise procedural geometry remains authoritative for flow. Gate turns, rising water, gentle reservoir shimmer and pulse splashes provide motion. Sound is synthesized locally, opt-in via first interaction, with mute; reduced motion removes camera and celebratory movement. Groundwater cutaway represents pores in rock rather than an underground cavern river.

## Scientific scope

Schematic original maps; tuned units and compressed time are not predictions or engineering recommendations. Surface transport conserves water, salt, contamination and reuse provenance. Aquifer storage/transport is a separate lumped abstraction, never a claim that all groundwater behaves as a surface river. Treatment does not certify potable water.
