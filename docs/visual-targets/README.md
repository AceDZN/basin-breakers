## Inspection and comparison checklist

Both 16:9 targets inspected visually on generation. Keep warm limestone, olive vegetation, turquoise water, copper wheel and ceramic-blue gate. Use a low-oblique readable whole-basin camera, actual channel depth and wet/dry response. Keep world about 85% of viewport and compact Hebrew controls. Identity gate must be large enough to read at play camera.

Target 1 succeeds at channels, material, finite-pulse atmosphere and gate identity. Its terrain is more photographic and expansive than a modest browser heightfield; retain physical relief and tonal hierarchy in a compact cutaway. Source-to-tank height ambiguity must not become physics in game.

Target 2 succeeds at distinct pipe patterns, quality labels and visible porous rock. It has scientific mistakes that must NOT be copied: generated single skid appears to produce reuse from seawater, groundwater is drawn as a broad void, and the level fraction text is malformed. Actual game must keep wastewater treatment and desalination inputs separate, represent aquifer storage in permeable cells, use correct Hebrew/24 labels, conserve all masses, and expose exact output routing. Images set art/camera/UI targets, not scientific authority.
