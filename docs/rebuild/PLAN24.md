# Full freeform flood-rescue campaign — authoring plan

The human expects a next mission after a rescue. The one-map prototype was a feasibility milestone, not the commissioned scope. All 24 missions below retain arbitrary digging/filling and freely placed finite levees. No authored solution patches, route-completion flags or retired quality quizzes return. Generated target 08 must be inspected before implementing the new campaign geometry.

Mission 1 preserves the current map and saved winning construction exactly. Subsequent missions vary source location, exposed courtyards, slope/crossfall, real relief/stone, earth/levee constraints and finite pulse timing. Green permeable areas transfer actual water into explicit porous-ground storage; a recharge goal is additional to home safety and sufficient actual coast passage.

## Proof and delivery gate

Each configuration gets an internal QA construction using public dig/wall actions, an actual complete-wave win, independent water/salt/contaminant/reuse ledger checks and a meaningful no-edit failure. Internal witnesses are never imported by product code. Record the actual receipt, home exposures, recharge, construction cost and flood schedule for all 24, and distinguish different geometry from a copied input order. Cross-section flux, changed-route transfer and delayed-pulse tests supplement final totals.

The existing recovery contracts remain mandatory on every map: arbitrary valid soft ground, no repeated excavation charge, free wall movement/rotation/removal, protected rock/source/homes, exact fill refund, pulse-only retry, actual-action Undo after reload and full construction constraints. Static art is not a hydraulic obstacle until it exists in the authoritative field.

The lead owns campaign navigation, progression and replay; art owns actual camera/terrain/object fit. QA owns configurations, model generalization and independent per-mission proof. Numerical passes are feasibility evidence; real pointer/touch play and direct human response remain separate.
