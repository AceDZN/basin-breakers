# Independent ordinary touch play — level 2

31 October 2026. Fresh isolated Chromium/Chrome context, 390×844 viewport, mobile/touch emulation, device scale 1. This is not a physical-phone performance test. The art owner knows the game's general mechanics and previously reviewed its visuals; this is not a blind new-player or fun-acceptance study.

Used real UI taps: campaign map → level 2. Chose the visible left side because the source was above it and all three homes were on the right. Drew one slightly crooked continuous trench from below the gate down the left soil to the coast using ordinary emulated touch events. No fixture route, model coordinates, projection hook, state mutation, time-step hook, stored solution or injected win was used. All path points were chosen from the rendered image. The visible earth counter changed from 155 to 101; no levees were placed.

Tapped Test and waited for the natural wave, capturing after about three seconds and after a further 6.5-second observation. The game displayed `הכפר ניצל!`, `כל שלושת הבתים בטוחים!` and the primary `השלב הבא`. Tapping that button loaded fresh level 3: source east, homes west, full 145 earth, disabled empty-history Undo, hidden outcome and Test restored. One attempt; no retry was necessary or tested in this small session. No browser/console errors were observed.
