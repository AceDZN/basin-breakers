# Rejected directions and the agency reset

31 October 2026. Keep the earlier images and exact prompts as history; do not present them as accepted targets for the new freeform prototype.

## What changed

1. **Original 24-level basin:** the human rejected the distant slab, weak visual hierarchy, complexity and lack of enjoyment. Technical solver/level checks did not establish a good game. The earlier visual review was too lenient about the gap from the photographic targets.
2. **Garden-first water toy, targets 01–03 and v2 edits:** the camera and action became clearer, but the human selected short flood-rescue puzzles instead of garden irrigation as the primary experience. These images are superseded direction history.
3. **Directed rescue, targets 04/05:** the human then rejected the implemented mechanic again: the player effectively clicks wherever the prompt appears, and the experience still does not work for them. Improving channel width, source/outlet visibility, phone fit and materials did not fix the core absence of agency. One authored sand-plug hotspot still encodes the plan in advance. These targets are now rejected as the core interaction contract, even where their material and source/home silhouettes remain useful references.

## Current direction: player-designed flood rescue

New targets 06/07 were generated from the lead's exact direction with two built-in calls and must be inspected before dependent implementation. The images, exact prompts, agency/scientific contract and unresolved source-enclosure/style challenges are recorded in `mockups/README.md`. No further model-generation credits were used for this reset.

- An initially **unbuilt open landscape** has a broad soft-soil valley and three downstream homes at risk. There is no precut correct bypass, preset safe destination, gold hotspot, hand cue or complete answer route.
- The player may dig continuously **anywhere within the broad visible soil**, and place a limited supply of roughly three to five levee blocks freely on valid ground. The material boundary with fixed stone is visible and consistent.
- The player chooses routing, storage and deflection. A finite approximately eight-second water event tests the resulting physical plan. Quick retry preserves the plan and encourages an actual revision.
- More than one materially different plan must work for physical reasons. A left bypass, a different deflection/storage arrangement or another genuine terrain solution must be testable without an answer-key flag.
- Water entering the actual house/courtyard regions causes failure. A prompted click, checkbox, decorative animation or preselected connection cannot substitute for actual terrain/levee influence on the field.

## Visual communication without giving the answer

Use a continuous warm terracotta editable valley, pale angular fixed limestone shoulders and three prominent houses lower downstream. The full soil region shares the same visual affordance. The held source, terrain gradient and house placement communicate risk; an arrow does not specify the response.

A small spade tool and a visible tray of available levee blocks explain available actions. The brush ring follows the player's chosen position on valid soil; it never snaps to an authored patch. Dark disturbed soil, cut depth and small shavings respond continuously to the actual drag. A release/test action and undo are enough for the first puzzle.

Target 06 must show the untouched valley. Target 07 may show one illustrative **player-authored** plan, with real cuts, storage and freely placed blocks; it is a later-state design target, not an overlay shown during initial planning. The caption must not imply that this is the sole correct plan.

## Acceptance reset

Do not declare the prototype acceptable because a large hotspot can be triggered or a physically conserved pulse reaches an authored sink. Verify that ordinary player input changes terrain at arbitrary valid positions, that blocks can be placed freely, and that at least two independently built plans protect the houses through the modeled flow. Then review the actual first minute with the human before treating campaign breadth as evidence of success. No student enjoyment or learning claim is established by these prior builds.
