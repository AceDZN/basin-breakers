# Basin Breakers — active freeform prototype

Visual thesis: sunlit terracotta earth between pale limestone shoulders, an enclosed turquoise reservoir uphill, and three small homes at risk below. The open landscape is the working surface; controls stay at the edge.

Interaction thesis: a drag cuts the player's own continuous trench; freely positioned stone levees change where the finite wave can flow; a short test reveals consequences and returns to retained construction. Excavation preview and commitment must agree. Fill restores any excavated soil and refunds its actual cost; Undo reverses excavation or fill, including during a wave. Retry restores water. The latest32 actual construction snapshots survive reload; legacy saves can be repaired freely with Fill.

The first minute starts in safe planning with the entire editable valley available. The player may test immediately to see the danger, draw a bypass, build a barrier, or combine them. No prompted excavation site, named route or answer socket. Full release and sufficient outflow are required alongside courtyard safety, so blocking everything is insufficient.

The next deliverable is one meaningful map, not a reskinned campaign. Independent physical alternatives prove construction choice; they do not prove fun. Direct human play remains the next design authority. Rejected versions remain in source and [history](docs/history/V1_GAME_IDENTITY.md).

Controls: shovel drag; choose/place levee; R or touch button rotates; select existing levee with shovel to move/remove; Space test/pause/resume; Z Undo;1/2/3tools; F fullscreen; Escape pause/close. Fixed whole-map view, including390×844. Sound opt-in; reduced motion; focus-loss pause. Schematic map/compressed time disclosed in footer.
